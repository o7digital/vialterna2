Mechanisms and procedure for exercising ARCO rights

ARCO rights refer to the following rights:

• Access: the right to know what personal data we process about you, as well as information about the conditions and general features of that processing.

• Rectification: the right to request, at any time, that inaccurate or incomplete personal data be corrected.

• Cancellation: the right to have the processing of your personal data cease, following its blocking and subsequent deletion.

• Opposition: the right to object, on legitimate grounds, to the processing of your personal data.

Exercising ARCO rights

You have ARCO rights to control your personal data, including updating it and, where appropriate, having it deleted.

To exercise any ARCO right, you must submit a request to our Personal Data Department through either of the following channels:

• Sending a request by email to protecciondedatos@vialterna.com.

• Sending a duly signed request to Montes Pirineos 720-B, Lomas de Chapultepec, Miguel Hidalgo, postal code 11000, Mexico City, Mexico.

Contents of an ARCO request

The request must contain or be accompanied by:

• Your full name and address, or another means of communicating the response to your request.

• A copy of a document proving your identity and, where applicable, the legal authority of anyone acting on your behalf. This requirement may be omitted if you provide information establishing that you are an employee of the Data Controller and identifying you beyond doubt.

• A clear and precise description of the ARCO right you wish to exercise and the personal data related to your request.

• Where applicable, any other information or document that helps us locate your personal data.

ARCO response periods

The Data Controller will respond to your request within 20 business days after it has been sent and received. If the request is granted, the Data Controller will implement it within 15 business days after communicating its response.

If the information and/or documentation provided in your request is incomplete, incorrect and/or insufficient, or if documents proving your identity or the relevant legal authority are missing, the Data Controller will ask you to correct and remedy these deficiencies so that the request can be processed. You will have 10 business days to respond and correct the request; otherwise, it will be considered not submitted.

Using electronic means to exercise ARCO rights authorizes the Data Controller to respond through the same means, unless you clearly and expressly indicate a different contact method in your request.

The right of cancellation is not absolute. The Data Controller must retain information to comply with various legal obligations. In such cases, it may be necessary to request cancellation from the entity that received your personal data.

Revocation of consent

In some cases, you may revoke your consent to the processing of your personal data. However, revocation cannot have retroactive effect: it cannot affect situations, procedures or transfers that took place before consent was revoked.

Consent also cannot be revoked where doing so would make it impossible to fulfill obligations arising from an existing legal relationship between you and the Data Controller, or would breach generally applicable public-order provisions requiring continued processing of personal data for a specified period.

You may send a request to revoke consent to protecciondedatos@vialterna.com, following the instructions applicable to exercising ARCO rights.

Restrictions on the use and disclosure of your personal data

You may limit the use or disclosure of your personal data by submitting a request to our Personal Data Department, following the procedure and instructions applicable to exercising ARCO rights.

The Data Controller has means and procedures to include certain personal data in its own exclusion lists to prevent disclosure. In such cases, the Data Controller will provide data subjects who request registration with the corresponding registration certificate.

Automated means of collecting personal data

Our website uses files known as "cookies" to facilitate browsing and communication. If you visit our website and/or submit information through it, the cookies we use allow us to collect, analyze and retain technical information relating to your browsing habits, your use of these communication channels and, where applicable, your geographic location.

You may disable cookies in your web browser. For more detailed information about cookies and how to prevent their installation, we recommend visiting www.allaboutcookies.org.

Please also be aware that disabling cookies or refusing the collection of your geographic location may prevent you from using certain sections or services of the website.
